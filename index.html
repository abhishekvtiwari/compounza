Understanding Investment Calculators

What is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) calculator helps you estimate the returns on your mutual fund investments made at regular intervals. It accounts for compounding and optional step-up increases.

What is an EMI Calculator?

An EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) calculator tells you exactly how much you'll pay each month on a loan. It also shows the total interest outgo over the loan's life.

Compound vs Simple Interest

Compound interest earns interest on interest — making it grow exponentially. Simple interest is linear. The difference becomes dramatic over long periods, which our calculators make visually clear.
